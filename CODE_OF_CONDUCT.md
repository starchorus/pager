# Code of Conduct

Starchorus is a piece of engineering. Contributions are judged on their merit and on nothing else:
whether the code works, whether it stays passive, and whether it is clear. This code applies in
issues, pull requests, reviews and every other space of the project.

## Principles

- **Merit.** A good patch is welcome from anyone; a poor one is declined whoever sends it. No one
  receives preference, or a penalty, for who they are.
- **Free and frank discussion.** Robust disagreement about code, design and direction is welcome.
  You may say plainly that an idea is wrong, provided you say why.
- **Politics stays outside.** The project takes no position on matters beyond its own work and
  asks nothing of contributors beyond their contribution. No statement of belief, affiliation or
  identity is expected, required or held against anyone.
- **Personal responsibility.** Everyone is answerable for their own words and their own code.
- **Courtesy.** Disagree with the argument, not the person. Plain manners cost nothing.

## Not tolerated

- Personal abuse, threats, intimidation or harassment, in public or in private, on any grounds.
- Publishing other people's private information without their consent, including real SSIDs, MAC
  addresses or capture files.
- Persistent disruption: repeating a settled argument, spam, or bringing outside causes into the
  project's spaces.

Opinions are not misconduct. Being disagreed with, or finding an argument unwelcome, is not in
itself a breach of this code.

## Reporting

Please report breaches to the maintainer through the 'Question or contact' issue form (see
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md#6-reporting-security-concerns) for how to report). Issues are public: if
you would rather not give details in public, ask there only for contact at first. The maintainer
treats reports in confidence.

## Consequences

Action is proportionate and follows the conduct, not the person. Someone accused of a breach is told
what it is and may answer before anything beyond a hidden comment is decided. The maintainer may
edit, hide or remove contributions, and may exclude people from taking part, temporarily or
permanently, where conduct warrants it.
